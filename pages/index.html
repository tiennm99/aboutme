aboutme

Let every site you build lead back to you.

One script tag adds a small floating bubble. Tap it and your blog, GitHub, CV and socials fly out around it.

Try it: the bubble is in the corner of this page. Drag it, or drop it on the target to hide it.
  • One line of HTMLWorks on any site: plain HTML, Jekyll, Hugo, WordPress.
  • PrivateNo cookies, no tracking, no visitor ids.
  • Fits inFollows the page's light or dark theme; English and Vietnamese.
  • FreeOpen source, MIT licensed, nothing to pay for.

1Write your links

Edit the example. Icons: home, blog, github, facebook, linkedin, x, youtube, email, telegram, cv, link.

2Choose where your links live

Save this as a file on your site

Upload it next to your pages, then enter its address. A path such as /profile.json works when it is on the same site; use a full https:// address when the file is elsewhere, and make sure that host allows cross-origin requests.
